The one call I cannot make from counts

12px is used 224 times and it is the size your users called tiny. From the source alone there is no way to tell which of those are body copy and which are dense labels inside a chip or a table cell.

Mechanical maps every 12 to label 13. Safe, nothing reflows, and the worst offenders at 9 and 10 still jump to 11. Promoted maps sentence text to body 15 and leaves genuine labels at 13. Much better to read, and it will push some tight rows onto a second line.

My recommendation is Promoted, run as a codemod plus a per-screen sweep for overflow. The mechanical version is cheaper but it will not settle the complaint.

Why the previous scale failed

AppTypography already has 15 named styles. They are used 19 times. The escape hatch, custom(size: n), is used 830 times.

A scale does not fail because it is wrong. It fails because typing around it is easier. The new tokens are shorter than the thing they replace, which is the only mechanism that actually holds: AppText.body(c) against AppTypography.custom(size: 14, weight: FontWeight.w400, color: c).
