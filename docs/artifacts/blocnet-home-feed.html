What actually changes

01
The two status cards collapse into one strip. They are the same kind of thing — a system telling you its state — and right now both are reporting nothing while taking 526px. The strip carries both, and either half expands back into a full card the moment it has something real to say.
02
The post header stops being a stack. Name, handle, project, tag and urgency currently occupy four separate rows before the body. They become two: who and when, then where and what kind. Same information, half the height.
03
Time gets short. "6 months ago" becomes "6mo". It is metadata, it is the least important thing in the header, and at full length it pushes the urgency pill onto its own line.
04
Hunter names stop truncating. "My Upda…" and "bless50i…" are both cut today. Widening the cell and dropping "My Updates" to "You" fixes both.
05
One pill, not three. feed_card.dart defines _FeedRoleChip, _ModernProjectChip and _TagPill privately, across four different radii. They all become AppPill.
06
The card gets split. 1,163 lines in one file, with three widget classes hiding at the bottom. Header, body and actions become their own files.

Nothing is removed. Alpha Radar, the Edge brief, urgency, tags, project attribution and every action survive. The only thing taken away is the amount of room a component gets when it has nothing to say, which is the difference between an app that feels dense and one that feels empty.
