Row anatomy — four conditions

Byline, title, chips. The byline is fixed and always present; the title always gets the largest type on the row; the chip line appears only when there is something true to put in it. Urgency is carried before reading, by the left edge and by how much vertical space the row takes. A row degrades by losing chips, never by losing structure.
