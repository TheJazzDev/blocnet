# Blocnet Investor Operating Memo

Last updated: March 8, 2026

## Executive Summary

Blocnet is building an intelligence and action platform for Web3 users.
Instead of forcing users to track projects across X, Telegram, YouTube, and Discord, Blocnet gives users one place to follow the projects they actually care about, receive timely updates, and act before important opportunities expire.

For a non-web3 investor, the core thesis is simple: crypto has plenty of information, but not enough accountable infrastructure that helps users turn information into action.
Blocnet is designed to close that gap.

The product is already at MVP stage with core mobile, backend, admin, and blockchain-connected workflow foundations in place.
The next 6 to 12 months are about launch hardening, app store distribution, token launch preparation, liquidity preparation, early monetization, and user growth.

This memo explains:

1. What Blocnet is solving
2. What is already built
3. What needs to be funded over the next 6 and 12 months
4. What recurring, annual, and one-time costs exist
5. What different funding amounts unlock
6. How the business can generate revenue and investor upside over time

## Problem

Crypto users miss opportunities because critical updates are spread across too many noisy channels.

Today, most project discovery happens through X, YouTube, Telegram, Discord, and word-of-mouth communities.
Those channels have three structural problems:

1. They are noisy
- Users see too much irrelevant information and miss the few updates that actually matter.

2. They are not accountable
- Influencers and content creators can post one-off content and move on.
- If users lose money, miss a deadline, or act on poor information, the platform rarely imposes consequences.

3. They are not action-oriented
- Most channels stop at awareness.
- They do not reliably carry users through the full lifecycle of project participation such as claims, KYC windows, launches, or governance deadlines.

The result is a broken experience:

1. Users miss high-value opportunities
2. Projects struggle to keep communities updated in a structured way
3. Valuable information gets buried in noise
4. Trust is weak because there is little contributor accountability

## Solution

Blocnet combines structured intelligence with action infrastructure.

The product is designed around four ideas:

1. Project-specific following
- Users follow the projects they are actually interested in.
- They receive timely updates tied to those projects instead of a generic social feed.

2. Continuous update ownership
- Blocnet contributors, called Ontas, are expected to guide users through the lifecycle of opportunities instead of publishing a single post and disappearing.

3. Contributor accountability
- Ontas are measured by trust and performance signals inside the product.
- Poor quality, misleading behavior, or weak follow-through can reduce reputation and eventually remove Onta status.

4. Intelligence-to-action flow
- Blocnet is not designed only to inform.
- It is designed to move a user from "I saw an update" to "I completed the action."

This is why Blocnet should not be viewed as another content app.
It is a structured participation layer for Web3.

## Revenue Model

Blocnet should be presented as a multi-line revenue business, not a single-token business.

### 1. Tipping Take Rate

What it is:
- the platform earns a percentage whenever users tip Ontas or contributors

When it activates:
- after community tipping volume becomes consistent enough to support a take rate without harming adoption

Leading metric:
- number of active tippers and total tipping volume

### 2. Withdrawal / Transaction Fees

What it is:
- the platform earns fees on wallet withdrawal or transfer-related operations

When it activates:
- as wallet usage grows and on-chain activity becomes regular

Leading metric:
- number of wallet transactions and monthly active wallet users

### 3. Paid Project Featuring

What it is:
- projects pay to be featured in high-visibility placements inside Blocnet

When it activates:
- once Blocnet has enough active users for project visibility to have clear value

Leading metric:
- active user base and campaign conversion performance

### 4. Premium Project Placement

What it is:
- projects pay to stay more visible for longer periods or across specific high-intent surfaces

When it activates:
- after the featured project model has early validation

Leading metric:
- repeat spend from projects and campaign retention

### 5. Hunter Onboarding Fee

What it is:
- a one-time fee for users who want Onta/Hunter status and access to monetizable contributor roles

When it activates:
- once Onta/Hunter status is meaningfully valuable and the platform can justify quality control around entry

Leading metric:
- number of qualified contributor applicants

### 6. Future Premium Intelligence Features

What it is:
- advanced intelligence, analytics, or premium access layers for serious users and teams

When it activates:
- after core retention is proven and free value is already strong

Leading metric:
- repeat weekly engagement from power users

## Risk and Mitigation

### Adoption Risk

Risk:
- users may remain on X, Telegram, and YouTube instead of moving behavior into Blocnet

Mitigation:
- focus on differentiated value: project-specific follows, accountable contributors, and action-oriented updates

### Solo-Founder Execution Risk

Risk:
- a single founder can become a throughput bottleneck

Mitigation:
- keep roadmap disciplined, prioritize high-leverage work, and use part-time contractor/community support only where funding justifies it

### Infrastructure Scaling Risk

Risk:
- free or low-tier infrastructure may fail under growth

Mitigation:
- move from free/starter tiers to paid tiers deliberately, track usage closely, and budget for progressive upgrades instead of reactive emergency moves

### Token and Liquidity Risk

Risk:
- weak token launch sequencing or poor liquidity conditions can damage trust

Mitigation:
- conservative token launch planning, phased liquidity strategy, and readiness checks before public execution

### Compliance and Platform Risk

Risk:
- app store, wallet, or platform policy requirements may slow launch

Mitigation:
- complete publishing and account setup early, keep requirements tracked, and avoid leaving compliance work until the last minute
