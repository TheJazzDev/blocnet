# Grant Acceptance Playbook (Founder Version)

Last updated: March 6, 2026

This playbook is designed for a solo founder running multiple grant opportunities at once.

## 1. Grant Strategy You Should Use

Treat grants as three buckets:

1. `Core Grant`
- Directly funds your core roadmap and launch.
- Example for Blocnet: BNB Chain grant tied to launch, token operations, liquidity, and ecosystem rollout.

2. `Strategic Grant`
- Funds a specific technical layer you would likely build anyway.
- Example for Blocnet: Reactive grant for event-driven automation if it improves core reliability and user action speed.

3. `Opportunistic Grant`
- Mostly ecosystem-alignment work with limited direct product upside.
- Accept only if effort is low and terms are very founder-friendly.

Rule: prioritize Core first, Strategic second, Opportunistic last.

## 2. Go/No-Go Checklist (Before You Accept Any Grant)

Answer each with `Yes` or `No`.

1. Does this grant fund work that is already on your 6-month roadmap?
2. Will this work improve retention, conversion, reliability, or revenue potential?
3. Can you deliver milestones without derailing core launch timeline?
4. Are milestone definitions objective and measurable?
5. Is payment timing clear and realistic for your cash flow?
6. Is the grant non-dilutive (no equity, no token obligations unless explicitly agreed)?
7. Are IP rights clearly yours?
8. Is there no exclusivity that blocks multi-chain or partner flexibility?
9. Are publicity/branding requirements manageable?
10. Are clawback/termination rules reasonable?
11. Are allowed expense categories clearly stated?
12. Can you support reporting requirements as a solo founder?
13. Does grant currency/asset volatility create manageable risk?
14. Are legal/KYC requirements acceptable to you?
15. Does this improve your investor story instead of complicating it?

Decision rule:
- `12-15 Yes`: Accept.
- `9-11 Yes`: Negotiate terms first.
- `0-8 Yes`: Decline.

## 4. Term Sheet / Agreement Red Flags

Do not sign until these are clear in writing:

1. `IP Ownership`
- You retain full ownership of product IP and code.

2. `Exclusivity`
- No chain exclusivity unless compensated significantly and strategically justified.

3. `Use Restrictions`
- Funds can be used for agreed forward-looking product growth expenses.

4. `Milestone Ambiguity`
- Avoid vague milestones like "improve ecosystem engagement" without metrics.

5. `Payment Timing`
- Exact trigger and expected payout window per milestone.

6. `Clawbacks`
- Limited to fraud/material breach, not subjective dissatisfaction.

7. `Publicity Rights`
- Grantor can announce participation, but cannot disclose sensitive details without approval.

8. `Termination`
- Clear cure period for issues before termination.

9. `Confidentiality`
- Confidential submission and reviewer process, especially for private repositories.

10. `Jurisdiction`
- Understand governing law and dispute process before signing.

## 5. What Grant Money Usually Can and Cannot Fund

Commonly accepted:
1. Engineering and integrations
2. Security testing/review
3. Infrastructure and reliability tooling
4. Technical documentation and developer onboarding
5. Program operations tied to milestone delivery

Often restricted or scrutinized:
1. Generic paid ads
2. Retroactive reimbursements for old work
3. Founder personal expenses not tied to project execution
4. Unclear discretionary spending

How to include growth spend safely:
- Tie growth cost to product adoption milestones.
- Example: partner integration support, launch distribution ops, developer enablement content.

## 6. Milestone Design Framework (How to Get Paid Faster)

Each milestone should include:

1. `Scope`
- Exactly what is built.

2. `Evidence`
- What you submit to prove completion.

3. `Acceptance Test`
- Binary pass/fail criteria.

4. `Timeline`
- Target date and buffer.

5. `Payment`
- Amount released on acceptance.

Use this format:

- Milestone: [name]
- Deliverable: [artifact or feature]
- Acceptance: [specific measurable criteria]
- Evidence: [links/screenshots/demo/video/tx hash/docs]
- Due: [date]
- Payment: [$ amount or token amount]

## 7. Grant Budgeting Model You Should Use

Use a forward-looking budget with 5 buckets:

1. Product engineering
2. Security and QA
3. Infra and reliability
4. Ecosystem integration/onboarding
5. Program operations

Budget rule:
- At least 70% should map directly to product capabilities or reliability.
- Keep 10-20% buffer for integration surprises and delays.

## 8. Token-Denominated Grant Risk Management

If grant pays in tokens:

1. Confirm payout denomination and conversion assumptions.
2. Decide treasury policy before first payout.
3. Define what percentage you immediately convert to stable assets.
4. Avoid overcommitting fixed USD expenses against volatile token receipts.
5. Keep milestone scope proportional to worst-case payout value.

## 9. Compliance, Finance, and Records (Do Not Skip)

Maintain a grant folder per program with:

1. Signed agreements and amendments
2. Milestone specs and acceptance records
3. Invoices/payment confirmations/tx evidence
4. Progress updates and communications log
5. Expense ledger mapped to each milestone

Operational minimum:
1. Separate wallet/account for grant flows when possible
2. Monthly grant P&L snapshot
3. Tax folder for grant receipts and conversions
4. Professional tax/legal review in your jurisdiction before filing

## 10. Reporting Cadence Template (Solo-Founder Friendly)

Send one concise update every 2 weeks:

1. What shipped
2. What is in progress
3. Risks/blockers
4. Metrics movement
5. Next milestone ETA
6. Support required from grant team

Keep updates objective and evidence-linked.

## 11. Multi-Grant Conflict Control

Before committing new work, verify:

1. No conflicting exclusivity clauses.
2. No double-counting the same milestone payout across grants.
3. Shared deliverables are explicitly approved by both parties.
4. Resource load is realistic for your team size.
5. Public messaging is consistent across all grant announcements.

## 12. Negotiation Questions You Should Ask Every Grantor

1. What are the exact allowed and disallowed expense categories?
2. Is payout in USD, stablecoin, native token, or mixed?
3. What is the milestone acceptance SLA?
4. What evidence format is required for approval?
5. Who is final approver for milestone sign-off?
6. What happens if milestone scope needs modification midstream?
7. Are there exclusivity or non-compete restrictions?
8. What IP rights do you request, if any?
9. What disclosure/publicity permissions are required?
10. Under what conditions can funds be clawed back?

## 13. Simple Acceptance Scorecard (Use Before Signing)

Score 1-5 each:

1. Strategic fit
2. Revenue/retention impact
3. Timeline impact
4. Delivery confidence
5. Cash flow clarity
6. Legal friendliness
7. Reporting burden
8. Brand/investor upside

Interpretation:
- 32-40: Strong accept
- 24-31: Accept after negotiation
- <=23: Decline

## 18. Quick Truths for Founders

1. A grant is not free if it consumes your roadmap focus.
2. The real cost of a grant is distraction plus reporting load.
3. The best grant is one that funds what you would build anyway.
4. Never trade strategic flexibility for small cash.
5. Milestone clarity is more important than headline amount.
