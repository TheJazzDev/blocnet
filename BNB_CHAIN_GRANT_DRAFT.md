## Brief Company Description
Blocnet is a BNB Chain focused crypto project intelligence and engagement platform. We turn noisy project updates into structured, actionable signals so users do not miss critical actions such as claims, launches, KYC windows, governance votes, and on-chain participation opportunities.

Our stack is already live at MVP level: a Flutter mobile app, a Next.js admin console, a NestJS backend, and a BNB Chain transaction engine. The engine combines custody orchestration, double-entry ledger accounting, deposit indexing, sweep automation, and withdrawal settlement with confirmation-based reconciliation.

The problem we solve is execution failure in Web3 discovery and participation. Most users miss value not because information is unavailable, but because it is fragmented, unstructured, and operationally hard to act on. Blocnet makes discovery and execution reliable for end users, gives project teams a measurable way to distribute high-signal updates, and includes a tokenized participation layer tied to BNB Chain liquidity.

## Grant Project Summary
We are applying for this grant to productionize and expand Blocnet as a BNB Chain native intelligence and transaction execution layer.

Grant scope:
1. Mainnet hardening of wallet settlement flows (deposit indexing, confirmations, treasury sweeps, withdrawals).
2. BNT token launch readiness, token distribution operations, and initial liquidity provisioning on BNB Chain DEX rails.
3. Developer-facing APIs and documentation so BNB Chain projects can publish structured updates and integrate action links.
4. Edge Intelligence upgrades (deterministic ranking plus optional ML enrichment) to improve relevance, urgency scoring, and conversion to on-chain actions.
5. Ecosystem integrations and growth campaigns with selected BNB Chain projects.

End result: a production-grade app and integration layer that drives more reliable user participation on BNB Chain and creates reusable tooling for ecosystem teams.

## Ecosystem Benefit
Blocnet benefits BNB Chain in three ways:
1. User activation: we convert passive content consumption into timely, trackable on-chain participation.
2. Token ecosystem growth: BNT launch and liquidity provisioning create an on-chain participation asset aligned with BNB Chain activity.
3. Developer leverage: we plan to open key integration surfaces (APIs, schemas, reference workflows) so builders can reuse them instead of rebuilding update-routing, ranking, and action-trigger infrastructure.

As adoption grows, Blocnet can become a distribution and execution layer that improves retention, campaign conversion, and transaction throughput across BNB Chain projects.

## Grant project innovation
Our innovation is the combination of three layers in one system:
1. Structured intelligence layer: deterministic ranking with reason codes (not black-box only).
2. Optional AI enrichment layer: deeper content analysis with graceful fallback when ML providers fail.
3. Financial execution layer: BNB Chain wallet settlement engine with ledger integrity, idempotency keys, confirmation-gated state transitions, and token-launch/liquidity operations.

Most products deliver either analytics or wallet rails; Blocnet links discovery, decisioning, and execution in one operational stack.

## Grant Project Competitive Landscape
Current alternatives are fragmented:
- Analytics platforms (e.g., Nansen): strong analytics, weak action routing for everyday retail workflows.
- Portfolio trackers (e.g., DeBank/Zerion): good asset visibility, limited project-update execution workflows.
- Quest/campaign platforms (e.g., Galxe/QuestN): campaign focused, less personalized ongoing intelligence.
- Telegram/Discord alpha channels: high noise, no reliability or structured accountability.

Blocnet differentiates by combining personalized, explainable project intelligence with direct execution rails and admin-grade operational controls on BNB Chain.
