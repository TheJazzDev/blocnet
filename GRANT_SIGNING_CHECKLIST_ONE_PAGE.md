# Grant Signing Checklist (1-Page)

Use this before signing any grant agreement.

## 1) Fast Decision Gate

Mark each item `Yes` or `No`.

1. The grant funds work already on my next 6-month roadmap.
2. The funded scope improves core product metrics (retention, reliability, conversion, revenue path).
3. Milestones are objective and measurable.
4. Payment triggers and payout timelines are explicitly written.
5. The team can deliver without delaying core launch.
6. Reporting workload is manageable for current team size.

Decision:
- `5-6 Yes` -> Proceed to legal review.
- `3-4 Yes` -> Negotiate first.
- `0-2 Yes` -> Decline.

## 2) Legal/Commercial Must-Haves

Do not sign unless all are clear in writing.

1. `IP`: You retain ownership of all product IP/code.
2. `Exclusivity`: No chain/platform exclusivity unless strategically justified and compensated.
3. `Grant Type`: Non-dilutive (no equity/token rights unless explicitly agreed).
4. `Clawback`: Limited to fraud/material breach, not subjective non-performance.
5. `Termination`: Clear cure period before cancellation.
6. `Confidentiality`: Sensitive docs/private repos protected.
7. `Publicity`: Any announcement rights require your approval for sensitive details.
8. `Jurisdiction`: Governing law and dispute process are acceptable.

## 3) Milestone Quality Check

Each milestone must include:

1. Deliverable (what is built)
2. Acceptance criteria (binary pass/fail)
3. Evidence (links/demo/tx hash/docs)
4. Due date
5. Payout amount

Red flag: vague outcomes like "increase adoption" without measurable criteria.

## 4) Budget & Spend Guardrails

Confirm allowed/disallowed expenses.

Typical safe categories:
1. Engineering/integration
2. Security/testing
3. Infrastructure/reliability
4. Documentation/partner onboarding
5. Program operations tied to milestone delivery

Typical risky categories:
1. Generic paid ads
2. Retroactive reimbursements
3. Personal/founder non-project expenses

Rule: >=70% of budget must map directly to product delivery or reliability.

## 5) Cash & Token Risk

If payout is in volatile tokens:

1. Define conversion policy before first payout.
2. Avoid fixed USD commitments against uncertain token value.
3. Keep milestone scope sized to worst-case payout value.

## 6) Multi-Grant Conflict Check

1. No conflicting exclusivity clauses across grants.
2. No double-counting same milestone for multiple payouts unless approved in writing.
3. Shared deliverables explicitly acknowledged by each grantor.

## 7) Pre-Sign Questions (Ask Grantor)

1. Exact allowed/disallowed spend categories?
2. Milestone acceptance SLA?
3. Who signs off each milestone?
4. Payout currency and timeline?
5. Scope-change process?
6. Clawback conditions?
7. IP and exclusivity terms?

## 8) Final Sign Rule

Sign only if:
1. Strategic fit is clear.
2. Legal terms are founder-safe.
3. Milestones are measurable.
4. Cash flow timing is workable.
5. Delivery load is realistic.

If any one fails, negotiate or decline.

## Blocnet Quick Position

- `BNB Grant`: Core grant (primary execution path).
- `Reactive Grant`: Strategic grant only if it funds core automation you would build anyway; no side-quest scope.
